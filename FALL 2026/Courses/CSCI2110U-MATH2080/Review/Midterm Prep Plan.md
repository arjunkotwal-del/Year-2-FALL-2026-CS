## Your weak spots (from the quizzes and practice sets)
1. **Expansion slips:** (2k+1)² = 4k²+4k+1, not 2k²+4k+1. Every coefficient gets squared.
2. **Choosing the proof technique:** direct vs. contraposition vs. contradiction. Use cases ONLY if the statement splits naturally.
3. **Negating quantified conditionals:** flip every quantifier, then ¬(a→b) becomes a∧¬b.
4. **Tautologies and precedence:** → is evaluated LAST. Anything ∨ T is a tautology.
5. **Quantifier rules:** each ∃ gets a NEW name, and never generalize (∀) from an ∃-element.
6. **Card puzzles:** read the letters carefully (V is a consonant).

## Definitions to know cold (they get used in almost every proof)
- Even: n = 2k. Odd: n = 2k+1. Rational: a/b with b≠0.
- p→q ≡ ¬p∨q ≡ ¬q→¬p. Converse and inverse are NOT equivalent.
- ¬(a→b) ≡ a∧¬b. De Morgan's: ¬(a∧b) ≡ ¬a∨¬b, ¬(a∨b) ≡ ¬a∧¬b.
- ¬∀xP ≡ ∃x¬P, ¬∃xP ≡ ∀x¬P.
