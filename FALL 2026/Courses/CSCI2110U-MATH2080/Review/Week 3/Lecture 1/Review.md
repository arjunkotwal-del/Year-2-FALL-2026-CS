# Review — Week 3 / Lecture 1 (Rules of Inference, §1.6 — course Lecture 4)

← [[../../../Week 3/Lecture 1/Notes|Full Notes]] | [[../../../Week 3/Lecture 1/Questions|Questions]] | ← [[../../../CSCI2110U-MATH2080 - Discrete Math]]

## What to review

1. **All 8 propositional rules of inference** — Modus Ponens, Modus Tollens, Hypothetical Syllogism, Disjunctive Syllogism, Addition, Simplification, Conjunction, Resolution. Know the symbolic shape of each, not just the name.
2. **Multi-step proof format**: numbered "Step / Statement / Reason" table, one rule cited per line.
3. **The 4 quantifier rules**: Universal/Existential Instantiation and Generalization — know which direction each goes (specific→general vs general→specific), and that Existential Instantiation must come BEFORE you can reuse that same constant with Universal Instantiation.
4. **The two fallacies**: affirming the conclusion, denying the hypothesis — both look like real rules but are contingencies, not tautologies. Know a concrete counterexample for each.
5. **Universal Modus Ponens/Tollens** — the combo move (quantifier rule + propositional rule chained together) used constantly without being named explicitly in later proofs.
