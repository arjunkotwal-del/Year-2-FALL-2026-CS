Q33 - truth tables (rows in order TT, TF, FT, FF)
  a) p AND NOT p:            F, F, F, F  (contradiction)
  b) p OR NOT p:              T, T, T, T  (tautology)
  c) (p OR NOT q) -> q:       T, F, T, F
  d) (p OR q) -> (p AND q):   T, F, F, T
  e) (p->q) <-> (NOT q->NOT p): T, T, T, T  (tautology -
     a conditional and its contrapositive always match)
  f) (p->q) -> (q->p):        T, T, F, T  (contingency)

Q35 (Section 1.2 - knights, knaves, spies)
  THERE IS NO VALID ASSIGNMENT - the puzzle is unsolvable.

  Reasoning: "I am not the spy" is true for everyone EXCEPT
  the actual spy. Now consider whoever holds the KNAVE role:
  a knave always lies, so their statement must be false. But
  "I am not the spy" is false only for the actual spy - and
  the knave is, by definition, a different person from the
  spy. So the knave's statement is actually TRUE (since the
  knave genuinely isn't the spy), contradicting the
  requirement that knaves always lie. This contradiction
  happens no matter who you assign as the knave, so no
  consistent assignment exists.
