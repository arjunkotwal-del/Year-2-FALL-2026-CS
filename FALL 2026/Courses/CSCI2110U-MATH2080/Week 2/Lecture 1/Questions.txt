Q1 - verify with truth tables

Q5 - distributive law (8 rows, p q r order TTT..FFF)
  q OR r:            T T T F T T T F
  p AND (q OR r):    T T T F F F F F
  p AND q:           T T F F F F F F
  p AND r:           T F T F F F F F
  (p AND q) OR (p AND r): T T T F F F F F
  Columns "p AND (q OR r)" and "(p AND q) OR (p AND r)"
  match in every row -> confirmed equivalent.

Q11 - prove tautologies (all six ARE tautologies)
  a) (p AND q) -> p:            T,T,T,T
  b) p -> (p OR q):              T,T,T,T
  c) NOT p -> (p -> q):          T,T,T,T
  d) (p AND q) -> (p -> q):      T,T,T,T
  e) NOT(p -> q) -> p:           T,T,T,T
  f) NOT(p -> q) -> NOT q:       T,T,T,T
  (rows in order TT, TF, FT, FF)

Q19 - is (NOT q AND (p -> q)) -> NOT p a tautology?
  YES. All four rows (TT,TF,FT,FF) evaluate to T. This is
  the logical form of MODUS TOLLENS ("if p->q and not-q,
  then not-p").

Q35 - show NOT equivalent
  (p -> q) -> r  and  p -> (q -> r)  are NOT equivalent.
  Counterexample: p=F, q=F, r=F
    (p -> q) -> r = (F->F) -> F = T -> F = F
    p -> (q -> r) = F -> (F->F) = F -> T = T
  Different results on the same input -> not equivalent.

TIME PERMITTING BONUS EXERCISES - answers
  1. (NOT p AND (p->q)) -> NOT q is NOT a tautology.
     Counterexample: p=F, q=T gives F.
  2. (p AND q) -> r  vs  (p->r) AND (q->r) are NOT
     equivalent. Counterexample: p=T,q=F,r=F gives T vs F.
  3. 16 different truth tables (2^4 possible output
     columns for a 4-row table).
  4. (p OR NOT q) AND (NOT p OR q) AND (NOT p OR NOT q) IS
     satisfiable - try p=F, q=F (all three clauses true).
  5. True when exactly two of p,q,r are true:
     (p AND q AND NOT r) OR (p AND NOT q AND r) OR
     (NOT p AND q AND r)

EXTRA - simplify NOT(NOT p OR q) OR (p AND q)
  NOT(NOT p OR q) = p AND NOT q   [De Morgan's + double negation]
  Expression becomes: (p AND NOT q) OR (p AND q)
  = p AND (NOT q OR q)             [distributive law]
  = p AND T                        [negation law]
  = p                              [identity law]
  FINAL ANSWER: just p
