## Topic outline
- **Wk 0:** Integration review
- **Wk 1 (Sept 8–11):** Data/populations/samples, histograms, continuous distributions
- **Wk 2 (Sept 14–18):** Discrete distributions, standard/nonstandard normal
- **Wk 3 (Sept 21–25):** Binomial distribution, measures of center/variability, quartiles/IQR/boxplots
- **Wk 4 (Sept 28–Oct 2):** **Term Test 1 (Oct 1)**; bivariate data, correlation, fitting a line
- **Wk 5 (Oct 5–9):** Least squares regression, residuals, probability/sample space/events, event operations
- **Reading week Oct 12–16:** no lecture
- **Wk 6 (Oct 19–23):** Conditional probability/independence, random variables/distributions
- **Wk 7 (Oct 26–30):** Mean/variance of RV, sampling distributions, Central Limit Theorem
- **Wk 8 (Nov 2–6):** Large-sample CIs (mean, one-sided, proportion), t-distribution
- **Wk 9 (Nov 9–13):** **Term Test 2 (Nov 12)**; hypothesis testing, Type I/II error, p-values, single-mean tests
- **Wk 10 (Nov 16–20):** Paired/independent two-mean tests, pooled t-procedure
- **Wk 11 (Nov 23–27):** Pooled t-procedure cont'd, full hypothesis review, ANOVA intro
- **Wk 12 (Nov 30–Dec 4):** R Studio — distributions, univariate analysis, correlation/regression, ANOVA
